Town hall and PTA Q&A, through everyone's own phone.

November 2026 · Covers what the published version (0.9.2) can do · 845.Lab

At briefings and general meetings in community centers, meeting halls or school gyms, the person asking a question uses their own smartphone as the microphone, so nobody has to carry a mic to their seat. Attendees just scan a QR code — there is no app to install and nothing to sign up for. Voices travel only over the venue's Wi‑Fi and are never sent to an outside server.

Sound familiar?

  • A volunteer runs back and forth across the room with the mic for every question
  • Questions from the back can't be heard, and “Could you repeat that?” becomes a refrain
  • A few people talk at length, and time runs out before others get a turn
  • Some people are uncomfortable raising a hand and speaking in front of a crowd
  • Writing up the record or minutes afterwards is a lot of work

What CloudMic Live does

Join by scanning a QR code

Attendees scan the QR code with their phone camera, enter a name and they're in. You can also print a QR poster for the entrance. To speak, they tap the big “Request to speak” button.

Every seat reaches the room speakers

Approved speakers' voices play from the PC through the venue's speakers, so a question from any seat is heard by everyone.

Fair turns with requests and time limits

Requests line up in order, and people waiting see “You are number 3 in line” on their phone. Set a speaking time limit to show the time remaining on screen. The chair can also mute from the PC.

A written question box

Questions that are hard to ask aloud can be sent in writing, anonymously if preferred. The most liked rise to the top, so you can start with what most people want to know.

Announcements and an agenda

Send a notice such as “We'll now take a short break” to every phone. Enter the agenda (items and times) and the screen shows the current item and the time remaining.

Recording and speech log (Monthly / Yearly plans)

Keep a recording and a speech log to help write up the minutes. While recording, attendees' screens show “Recording”. Recordings are saved on the PC and never sent anywhere.

How it works (3 steps)

1

Create an event on the PC

Launch the app on a Windows PC connected to the venue's Wi‑Fi and create an event. Connect the PC's audio to the venue's speakers.

2

Attendees scan the QR at the door or on screen

Put a printed QR poster at the entrance, or show the code on the screen. Attendees simply scan it with their phone camera.

3

Approve speakers in turn during Q&A

The chair approves people who tapped “Request to speak”, one at a time, from the PC. Only approved voices are played in the room.

What you need

  • One Windows 10 / 11 PC for the host (the app is a free download from Microsoft Store)
  • The PC and everyone's devices on the same Wi‑Fi. No internet connection is needed. If the venue's Wi‑Fi stops devices from reaching each other, you can use the PC's Mobile hotspot instead.
  • Speakers to play voices (the PC's own speakers, or the room's sound system connected to the PC)
  • Attendees' smartphones (Safari on iPhone, Chrome on Android). It is a good idea to keep one regular microphone on hand for anyone without a smartphone or unsure how to use it.

Pricing

Free for up to 5 participants. For bigger groups: Event Pass US$4.99 (24 hours), Monthly US$9.99 or Yearly US$69.99. Participants are always free.

Common questions

Can people who aren't comfortable with smartphones use it?

Scanning the QR code opens a page in the browser; they enter a name and tap “Join”. To speak, they tap “Request to speak”. The first time they connect, the browser shows a security warning and asks for microphone permission, so it helps if the chair walks everyone through the steps once at the start (how to get past the warning is in the FAQ).

What if the venue has no Wi‑Fi?

It works as long as the PC and attendees' phones are on the same Wi‑Fi; no internet connection is needed. If the venue has no Wi‑Fi, bring a Wi‑Fi router or use the PC's Mobile hotspot. Try it once at the venue before the meeting.

Is what people say sent anywhere?

No. Voices and event data travel only over the venue's Wi‑Fi. Even when you use recording, the file is saved only on the host's PC.
