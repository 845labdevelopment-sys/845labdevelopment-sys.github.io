Turn students' phones into classroom microphones.

November 2026 · Covers what the published version (0.9.2) can do · 845.Lab

With one teacher PC and the phones or tablets students already have, anyone can speak from any seat in a classroom or lecture hall. Students scan a QR code and it opens in the browser — no app to install, no account to create. Voices travel only over the room's Wi‑Fi and are never sent to an outside server.

Sound familiar?

  • There is one microphone, and you walk it over to every student who speaks
  • Students in the back are too quiet for the rest of the room to hear
  • Some students never raise a hand, so the same few voices fill every discussion
  • Collecting the whole class's ideas by show of hands or on paper takes too long

What CloudMic Live does

Speak right from the seat

A student taps “Request to speak”; when you approve on the PC, their voice plays from the PC's speakers. Students waiting their turn see “You are number 3 in line” on their phone.

Nomination and random pick

In nomination mode you invite a chosen student to speak. Random pick can favor students who have not spoken yet.

Polls and a word cloud for everyone

With a poll or a word cloud of short answers, every student can respond without speaking up. Show the results large on the projector.

A written question box

Questions that are hard to ask out loud can be sent in writing, anonymously if a student prefers. The most liked questions rise to the top, and you can pin one on the screen.

Mute and speaking time

Mute one student or everyone at once, so only the right person is live. Set a speaking time limit and show the time remaining on screen.

Attendance and roster matching

Students tap “Present” and attendance is taken. Load a roster (CSV) to check it against who joined. Exporting attendance with the roster is a Monthly / Yearly plan feature.

How it works (3 steps)

1

Create an event on the teacher's PC

Launch the app on a Windows PC and create an event for the class. Connect the PC to the same Wi‑Fi as the students' devices.

2

Students scan the QR code

Show the join QR code on the projector, or put up a printed QR poster. Students scan it with their camera, enter a name and join.

3

Approved students are heard

When a student taps “Request to speak” and you approve, their voice plays through the classroom speakers.

What you need

  • One Windows 10 / 11 PC for the host (the app is a free download from Microsoft Store)
  • The PC and everyone's devices on the same Wi‑Fi. No internet connection is needed. If the venue's Wi‑Fi stops devices from reaching each other, you can use the PC's Mobile hotspot instead.
  • Speakers to play voices (the PC's own speakers, or the room's sound system connected to the PC)
  • Students' devices: phones or tablets with a browser and a microphone. Students' own devices (BYOD) and school-issued devices both work, though managed school devices may not be able to connect, depending on their settings.
  • Nice to have: a projector or large display (for poll results and the QR code)

Common questions

Does it work on school Wi‑Fi?

Yes, as long as the teacher's PC and the students' devices are on the same Wi‑Fi and the network lets devices talk to each other. If it doesn't, students can connect to the PC's Mobile hotspot instead. Before class, run the app's “Venue check (rehearsal)” to measure how many people that PC and Wi‑Fi can handle reliably.

Are students' voices recorded?

Not normally. Only when a teacher on the Monthly or Yearly plan turns on recording is audio saved, as a file on the teacher's PC. While recording, students' screens show “Recording”.

How do I use it with a whole class (more than 5)?

Up to 5 participants can join for free. For larger classes, choose the Event Pass (24 hours) or a Monthly or Yearly plan. Students are always free.
