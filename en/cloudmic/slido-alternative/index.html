CloudMic Live vs Slido

Facts checked November 2026 · prices quoted from public listings and resellers (street prices) and may change · 845.Lab

Slido collects written questions, polls and quizzes from an audience over the internet and is widely used in webinars and conferences. CloudMic Live is about the spoken question: participants' phones become microphones, the host approves who is live from a Windows PC, and everything runs on the venue's Wi-Fi. If your audience is remote, Slido is in its element; if your audience is in the room and you want to hear them, CloudMic Live is built for that.

Which one fits your room

Slido is the better fit when…

  • Your audience is remote or hybrid and joins over the internet.
  • You want written, anonymous questions with upvoting, or quizzes and analytics.
  • You present from PowerPoint or Google Slides and want polls inside the deck.
  • The audience is large (hundreds or thousands) and spread across locations.
  • Your organisation already licenses Slido through Cisco or Webex.

CloudMic Live is the better fit when…

  • The audience is in the room and you want to hear them, not read their questions aloud.
  • The venue has no reliable internet, or the discussion must stay in the room.
  • You want the host to control the floor — who speaks, for how long, and who is next.
  • You want to pay per event (a few dollars) or a small monthly fee, rather than an annual contract.
  • You run classes, workshops or staff meetings from one laptop.

What it costs

Slido: paid plans are billed annually (there is no monthly plan), and one-time event plans are available. On the Japanese pricing page in November 2026, annual plans started at ¥135,000 per year and one-time plans at ¥30,000 (7 days). US-dollar pricing varies by plan and region; see slido.com/pricing. The free tier allows up to 100 participants per event with 3 polls.

CloudMic Live: free for up to 5 participants with all core features. For bigger rooms, an event pass is US$4.99 for 24 hours, or US$9.99 a month / US$69.99 a year (adds recording, exports and reports). Participants are always free. Purchases go through Microsoft Store.

In one sentence

Slido is for audiences that type, wherever they are; CloudMic Live is for audiences in the room who would rather talk, and it needs no internet to do it.

Common questions

Does CloudMic Live have text questions and polls too?

Yes. A question box, polls and a word cloud are included, shown on the host screen and the projector view. They are simpler than Slido's, and they work without internet.

Can I use Slido and CloudMic Live together?

Yes. Some hosts keep Slido for remote attendees and written questions, and use CloudMic Live so that people in the room can speak. CloudMic Live can send the room's audio to the Teams or Zoom meeting at the same time.

Is there a monthly plan?

Yes: US$9.99 a month, or a US$4.99 pass that covers 24 hours for a single event.
