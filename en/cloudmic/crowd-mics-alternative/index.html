CloudMic Live vs Biamp Crowd Mics

Facts checked November 2026 · prices quoted from public listings and resellers (street prices) and may change · 845.Lab

Both products let people in the audience speak through their own phones while a moderator controls who is live. Crowd Mics is a professional AV system built around a dedicated receiver (the ATOM) and a participant app, sized for halls of up to 1,000 people. CloudMic Live runs on one Windows PC, participants join from the browser, and everything stays on the venue's Wi-Fi. They are built for different rooms, and this page tries to show which is which.

Which one fits your room

Biamp Crowd Mics is the better fit when…

  • You run large events (hundreds to 1,000 people) with an AV team.
  • You need HDMI output to house displays and pro audio connectors into a mixing console.
  • Remote attendees must be able to speak as well as people in the hall.
  • Your audience is multilingual and you want 20+ interface languages.
  • Budget allows a dedicated device and a managed Wi-Fi network.

CloudMic Live is the better fit when…

  • The room is a meeting room, classroom or lecture room, not a hall.
  • You cannot ask participants to install anything — they scan a code and talk.
  • There is no internet at the venue, or the content must not leave the room.
  • You want to decide on the day of the event and pay a few dollars, not make a capital purchase.
  • You also want polls, a question box, random pick and a projector view from the same PC.

What it costs

The participant and moderator apps are free, and in-room use needs no license. Remote participation uses the separate Crowd Mics Online license (reseller listings showed an annual device license of about US$1,980 and a single-use license of about US$110). CloudMic Live: free for up to 5 participants with all core features. For bigger rooms, an event pass is US$4.99 for 24 hours, or US$9.99 a month / US$69.99 a year (adds recording, exports and reports). Participants are always free. Purchases go through Microsoft Store.

In one sentence

Crowd Mics is a hall-scale AV system with an app and a dedicated receiver; CloudMic Live is the same idea shrunk to one PC and a QR code, for rooms where nobody wants to install anything.

Common questions

Is CloudMic Live a drop-in replacement for Crowd Mics?

No. If you depend on HDMI output, pro audio connectors, remote speakers or 1,000-seat capacity, Crowd Mics is the right tool. CloudMic Live covers the smaller rooms where a dedicated receiver would be more than you need.

Does CloudMic Live need any special network?

Only a Wi-Fi network that lets the phones reach the host PC. If the venue Wi-Fi isolates devices, the PC's Mobile hotspot works as a fallback. No internet connection is needed at any point.

Can several people talk at once?

The host approves speakers from a request queue and can mute anyone. The design keeps the room orderly rather than opening many microphones at the same time.
