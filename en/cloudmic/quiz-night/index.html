Quiz night buzzers, on everyone's phone.

November 2026 · Covers what the published version (0.9.2) can do · 845.Lab

Players' smartphones become both buzzer and microphone. The buzz-in order appears on the PC automatically, and when the host approves, the answering player's voice plays through the venue speakers. The scoreboard can go up large on the projector. Players just scan a QR code — there's no app to install.

Sound familiar?

  • Getting a buzzer for every player is a hassle
  • It's hard for the host to tell by eye who buzzed first
  • Keeping score on paper or a whiteboard means constant tallying
  • The answering player's voice doesn't reach the back of the room

What CloudMic Live does

Every phone is a buzzer

Buzz-in order is recorded and ranked automatically. Near-simultaneous presses are shown as a “Tie”, and the host sets how close counts as a tie (to allow for Wi‑Fi delay).

Fouls

Mark a wrong answer as a “Foul” and that player can't buzz again on that question. Moving on to the next question clears the ranking and fouls and opens the buzzer again.

Scoreboard and team mode

The host adds points on the PC and can show the scoreboard on the screen. Each player sees their own score on their phone. Team mode, which splits players into teams and totals their scores, is an Event Pass, Monthly or Yearly plan feature.

Screen view

A separate projector window shows the buzz-in ranking, the scoreboard, the current speaker and the join QR code, large enough for the room.

Applause and cue sounds

When players tap 👏, applause plays from the venue speakers. The host can also play “Start”, “End” and “Bell” cue sounds from the PC.

Random pick and polls

Use random pick for prize draws; it can favor people who haven't been picked yet. Polls and a word cloud are there too, for taking the room's opinion.

How it works (3 steps)

1

Create an event and open the screen view

Launch the app on a Windows PC and create an event. Put the screen view window on the projector.

2

Players scan the QR code

Players scan the QR code on the screen with their phones to join. For team play, split them into teams at this point.

3

Read a question and open the buzzer

Read the question, then open the buzzer. Players line up in the order they pressed: let number one answer, add the points and move on to the next question.

What you need

  • One Windows 10 / 11 PC for the host (the app is a free download from Microsoft Store)
  • The PC and everyone's devices on the same Wi‑Fi. No internet connection is needed. If the venue's Wi‑Fi stops devices from reaching each other, you can use the PC's Mobile hotspot instead.
  • Speakers to play voices (the PC's own speakers, or the room's sound system connected to the PC)
  • Nice to have: a projector or large display (for the ranking and the scoreboard)

Pricing

Free for up to 5 participants. For bigger groups: Event Pass US$4.99 (24 hours), Monthly US$9.99 or Yearly US$69.99. Participants are always free.

Common questions

How is buzz-in order decided?

Presses are ranked in the order they reach the host's PC from players' phones. Because a busy Wi‑Fi network can add small delays, the host can set a window within which very close presses count as a tie (or turn it off).

How many players can join?

Up to 5 players can join free. For bigger groups, the Event Pass (US$4.99, 24 hours) removes the participant limit. How many people can play reliably depends on the PC and Wi‑Fi, so run the app's “Venue check (rehearsal)” before the event.
