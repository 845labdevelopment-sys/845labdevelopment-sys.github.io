Tap the back of your iPhone twice to send your copy to your PC

With Back Tap and a shortcut · November 15, 2026

With iPhone's Back Tap, you can send the text you just copied to a PC on the same Wi‑Fi by tapping the back of the phone twice. No need to find and open AirBoard every time.

You set it up once, and it takes about two minutes.

What you need

  • AirBoard on both your iPhone and your PC, connected once
  • An iPhone that supports Back Tap (iPhone 8 or later, iOS 14 or later)
  • The Shortcuts app (built in; if you deleted it, you can get it again from the App Store)

1. Make the shortcut (once)

  1. Open Shortcuts, go to All Shortcuts, and tap + at the top right
  2. Tap Add Action, then search for and choose Open URLs
  3. Enter airboard://send-clipboard as the URL
  4. Name it "Copy to PC" and save it

2. Assign it to Back Tap

  1. Open Settings › Accessibility › Touch › Back Tap
  2. Choose Double Tap (or Triple Tap), then pick "Copy to PC"

Try it

  1. Copy the text you want to send
  2. Tap the back of your iPhone twice
  3. The shortcut shows for a moment, AirBoard opens, and the text lands on your PC's clipboard. Press Ctrl+V on the PC to paste

If you are asked to "Allow Paste": iPhone asks before an app reads text copied in another app. Tap Allow Paste to send it. To stop being asked every time, go to Settings › Apps › AirBoard › Paste from Other Apps and choose Allow (on iOS 17 and earlier, pick AirBoard straight from the Settings list).

If it does not work

  • Make sure AirBoard is running on the PC and that the PC is on the same Wi‑Fi as your iPhone.
  • A thick case can make Back Tap less responsive. Triple Tap also cuts down on accidental taps.
  • You can send the same way with AirBoard's Send to PC widget on the Home Screen or its Send to PC button in Control Center.
