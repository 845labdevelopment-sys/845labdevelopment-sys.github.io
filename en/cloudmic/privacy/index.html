In short: audio and event data stay between the host's PC and the participants' devices, and nothing is sent to the developer. Audio is normally not stored; only a paid plan's recording feature saves a file, and only on the host's PC.

What the app does

CloudMic Live turns smartphones on the same Wi-Fi network into microphones and lets the host manage who speaks (requests, permissions, mute) from a PC. The host app runs a small server on the PC; participants connect to it from their phone's browser.

Information handled

  • Display names entered by participants
  • Microphone audio while a participant is actively speaking
  • Event state (speech requests, permissions, mute, reactions, buzzer order)
  • Connection details (event ID, optional passcode, a random temporary identifier created in the browser to tell participants apart)

Storage and transmission

All of this stays between the host's PC and the participants' devices on the local network. Nothing is sent to the developer or to third-party services. Audio is normally played back live and not stored. Only when the host uses the recording feature of a paid plan is an audio file saved, and only on the host's own PC; nothing is sent to the developer or any server. While recording, every participant's screen shows a "Recording" indicator. The speech log (who spoke when and for how long) likewise stays on the host's PC. Event state is kept temporarily in the host's browser for the duration of the event. The passcode is checked only on the host's PC and is never distributed to participants.

Information not used

No location, contacts, photos or account data. No analytics or advertising SDKs. The notice shown on the participant screen in the free tier is bundled with the app and loads nothing from the internet.

Microphone permission

Participants grant microphone access in their browser and can revoke it at any time in their device settings. The microphone is used only between "Start speaking" and "Stop speaking".

Purchases

Paid plans are processed by the store that distributes the app. The developer never receives payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at any particular age group and collects no personal information.

Changes and contact

Changes will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: 845labsupport@gmail.com.
