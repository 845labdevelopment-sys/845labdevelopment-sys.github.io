Privacy

What you share stays between your devices.

Text and files handled by AirBoard never pass through a server on the internet. Ads in the mobile apps are served by Google AdMob, but what you share is never used for ads and never sent to Google.

FAQ

Questions

Is it free?
Yes, every feature is free. The phone and tablet apps show ads, which you can remove once with Settings → Remove ads. The Windows and Mac apps have no ads.
Does it only work on the same Wi‑Fi?
Yes. AirBoard devices talk to each other directly inside the same Wi‑Fi / LAN. It does not send to devices elsewhere over the internet — which also means nothing you share is left on an outside server.
How many devices can I connect?
Use a PC or another device as a Hub and connect several devices at once. Text and files also reach devices connected behind another device, and every device on the same Hub appears on the map.
Will it work on office or school Wi‑Fi?
Some networks block devices from talking to each other, so AirBoard may not connect. In that case, connect your iPhone to Windows Mobile hotspot, or your PC to the iPhone's Personal Hotspot (set the network to Private on the PC). See how. If your workplace doesn't allow hotspots, ask your administrator.
How do I contact you?
Email 845labsupport@gmail.com. If something isn't working, including the connection diagnostics from the app's Settings helps us find the cause faster.
