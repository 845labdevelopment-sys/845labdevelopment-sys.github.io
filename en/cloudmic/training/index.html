Training, meetup and seminar Q&A, through attendees' phones.

November 2026 · Covers what the published version (0.9.2) can do · 845.Lab

In meeting rooms and seminar venues, attendees' smartphones become the Q&A microphones. The trainer or organizer approves speakers on the PC screen and handles mute and timing from there. You can also send the voices of in-room questioners to an online meeting (Teams or Zoom) at the same time. Voices travel only over the local Wi‑Fi and are never sent to an outside server.

Sound familiar?

  • Passing the mic around during Q&A eats into the session
  • People joining online can't hear the questions asked in the room
  • Attendees hold back, wondering whether their question is worth asking
  • Taking attendance and writing the post-session report take time

What CloudMic Live does

Q&A by request

People with a question tap “Request to speak” and the trainer approves them in turn. Those waiting see their place in line. Speaking time limits and individual or global mute keep the session on track.

In the room and online at once

Set up “Output” on the PC to play in-room questioners' voices on the venue speakers and send them to Teams or Zoom. Audio reaches the meeting app through a free virtual audio cable. Online attendees cannot speak through their phones.

Question box and polls

Written questions can be sent anonymously and are sorted by likes. Pin a chosen question on the screen. Check understanding with a poll or a word cloud.

Attendance and roster matching

Attendees tap “Present” and attendance is taken. Load a roster (CSV) to check it against who joined. Exporting is a Monthly / Yearly plan feature.

Agenda and screen view

Enter the program and times, and the projector shows the current item and the time remaining. Attendees can send the host a private note, such as “The sound is too quiet”, without speaking up.

Recording, speech log and reports (Monthly / Yearly plans)

Recording, a speech log, attendance export and automatic event reports make the post-training write-up easier. While recording, attendees' screens show “Recording”.

How it works (3 steps)

1

Create an event on the trainer's PC

Launch the app on a Windows PC on the same Wi‑Fi as attendees. If you are also running an online meeting, add the virtual audio cable under “Output”.

2

Attendees scan the QR code

Just show the join QR code on your first slide or on the screen. Attendees open it in their phone's browser, enter a name and join.

3

Approve during Q&A

Approve someone who tapped “Request to speak” and their voice plays on the venue speakers — and, in a hybrid session, reaches the meeting app too.

What you need

  • One Windows 10 / 11 PC for the host (the app is a free download from Microsoft Store)
  • The PC and everyone's devices on the same Wi‑Fi. No internet connection is needed. If the venue's Wi‑Fi stops devices from reaching each other, you can use the PC's Mobile hotspot instead.
  • Speakers to play voices (the PC's own speakers, or the room's sound system connected to the PC)
  • For hybrid sessions: a virtual audio cable (free software), with the meeting app's microphone set to that cable. The steps are under the “?” next to “Output” in the app.
  • Nice to have: a projector or large display (for the QR code, questions and the agenda)

Common questions

What if it won't connect on the office Wi‑Fi?

Corporate Wi‑Fi often blocks devices from talking to each other for security. In that case, attendees' phones can connect to the PC's Mobile hotspot instead; check with your IT team that this fits your company's policy. Before the session, the app's “Venue check (rehearsal)” measures how many people can connect reliably.

Could the training content leave the room?

Voices and event data travel only over the same Wi‑Fi and are never sent to the developer's servers or anywhere else. Recordings, when used, are saved only on the host's PC. In a hybrid session, the audio you pass to the meeting app is carried by that meeting app's own service.
